### 🔷 About Me

I'm an Electrical Engineering (Honours) graduate from **Mirpur University of Science and Technology (MUST)**, focused on **power systems, smart grids, and renewable energy integration**. My final-year thesis built an IoT-based automated irrigation system that cut water usage by up to 40% through sensor-driven, autonomous control logic — the kind of hands-on systems thinking I now bring to power engineering problems.

I'm expanding from classical power systems into **data-driven and AI/ML-assisted approaches** for energy networks — using Python for power-flow visualization and analysis, and exploring prompt engineering and generative AI as tools for engineering research and automation. My background spans **grid infrastructure (WAPDA hydro), mission-critical data center power systems (UPS/battery banks), and embedded control (ESP32, sensors, relays)**.

**Open To:**
- 🎓 Graduate research / MS positions in Power Systems, Smart Grids, or AI applied to Energy
- 🔬 Research Assistant / Teaching Assistant roles
- ⚡ Entry-level roles in power systems, grid analytics, or energy data engineering
