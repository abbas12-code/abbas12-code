## ⚡ About Me

I am a highly motivated **Electrical Engineering (Honours)** graduate specializing in power systems engineering, smart grids, and renewable energy integration. My core passion lies at the intersection of electrical infrastructure and modern computer science: applying **Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning** to optimize smart grids, forecast renewable generation, and secure future microgrids.

* 🧠 **AI/ML for Energy:** Actively researching neural networks, regression models, and data-driven optimization for power flow and predictive maintenance.
* 🔋 **Power Engineering:** Experienced in load flow analysis, protection coordination, and high-voltage substation engineering.
* 🛠️ **System Design & Simulation:** Proficient in modeling systems using **ETAP**, **MATLAB**, and **AutoCAD Electrical**.
* 🎯 **Goal:** Bridging traditional power systems engineering with intelligent computing to build sustainable, resilient, and fully automated grids.
