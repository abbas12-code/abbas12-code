I'm an Electrical Engineering graduate from **MUST**, specializing in **Power Systems, Renewable Energy, and Smart Grid technologies**, with a strong and growing interest in applying **Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning** to modern power networks. My final year thesis focused on an **IoT-based automated irrigation system**, where I combined embedded sensing, control logic, and optimization to cut water usage by up to 40%.

Through internships at a **data center infrastructure team (DWP Group)** and **WAPDA Mangla Hydel Power Station**, I've worked hands-on with UPS systems, battery banks, SCADA, protection relays, and large-scale hydroelectric generation — building a solid foundation in real-world power system operations. I pair this with scientific computing in **Python (NumPy, Pandas, Matplotlib, SciPy)** and **MATLAB** to model, simulate, and visualize power system behavior.
