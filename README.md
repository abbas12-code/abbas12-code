## About

I am an **Electrical Engineering graduate** focused on building reliable, intelligent, and sustainable energy systems. My engineering interests span **power-system analysis, renewable-energy integration, smart grids, microgrids, protection, embedded control, scientific computing, and AI-assisted energy optimization**.

My professional experience includes supporting mission-critical **UPS systems, battery banks, PDUs, and electrical documentation for data-center infrastructure**, as well as working around the generation, protection, SCADA, and grid-integration systems of a **1,000+ MW hydroelectric power station**.

I develop engineering solutions using **Python, MATLAB, ETAP, AutoCAD Electrical, PLC concepts, embedded sensors, and data visualization tools**. My final-year thesis combined sensing, intelligent control, actuation, and real-time monitoring in an autonomous irrigation platform that reduced water consumption by up to **40%**.

I approach engineering with a **full-stack product mindset**: acquire trustworthy data at the sensor edge, implement dependable control logic, analyze system behavior, visualize actionable information, and translate results into maintainable engineering documentation. I am particularly interested in applying **artificial intelligence and machine learning** to forecasting, control, optimization, fault analysis, and decision support for future energy networks.
